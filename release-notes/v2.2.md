<summary><h2>🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia</h2></summary>

### ⚡ Peningkatan

- **Download live yang sedang berjalan**: pilih rekam dari awal live atau mulai dari detik ini. Rekaman berjalan sampai live selesai atau kamu menekan "Berhenti & simpan"
- **Tema terang dan gelap** di kedua aplikasi (bawaan: YouTube Lumi terang, YT Music Lumi gelap)
- **Warna biru Lumi** (aktif secara bawaan, bisa dimatikan di Setelan): tampilan dominan biru seperti logo Lumi, termasuk garis putar, Shorts, dan logo
- **Pop up offline**: muncul kecil di bawah saat internet putus, di aplikasi maupun mode PiP, lalu "Kembali online" saat tersambung lagi
- **Lencana "Khusus anggota"** pada video yang hanya tersedia untuk anggota channel, dan video serta Shorts khusus anggota tidak lagi muncul di Beranda
- **Setelan dalam satu layar**, tanpa perlu scroll
- **Watch & Listen Together**: secara bawaan hanya pembuat room yang memilih video/lagu, dengan opsi agar anggota room juga bisa memilih
- Judul video tampil di layar penuh bersama tombol kontrol
- Tombol **Tutup** di notifikasi pemutar video dan lagu, sekaligus menutup jendela PiP
- YouTube Lumi: titik tiga di playlist kini berisi Download dan Bagikan, seperti YT Music Lumi
- **Hentikan download playlist** dari aplikasi (halaman playlist dan halaman Download) maupun notifikasi; lagu/video yang sudah selesai tetap tersimpan
- YT Music Lumi: tombol cari kini di bilah atas dan foto profil di menu bawah (tab Anda), seperti YouTube Lumi
- YT Music Lumi: kotak kategori di Jelajahi senada dengan Koleksi
- YT Music Lumi: pilihan download kini dimulai dari audio, baru video, dan "Download semua" playlist kini menyimpan audio, bukan video
- Panduan "Atur ulang link" kini berupa langkah-langkah yang jelas dan tidak terpotong
- **Hapus dari histori tontonan** lewat titik tiga di Histori. Shorts di Histori kini juga punya titik tiga: Tambahkan ke playlist, Download, Bagikan, dan Hapus dari histori tontonan
- Daftar video terkait kini diberi penanda Shorts dan Video, seperti di Histori
- **Subtitle Bahasa Indonesia**: bila video tidak punya subtitle Indonesia, subtitle aslinya diterjemahkan otomatis ke Bahasa Indonesia; bila video tidak punya subtitle sama sekali, muncul keterangan "tidak punya subtitle". Judul dan audio video tetap asli
- Menghapus penelusuran di YouTube Lumi dan YT Music Lumi (tekan lama di histori penelusuran) kini ikut menghapusnya dari histori penelusuran YouTube dan YouTube Music asli
- Layar perkenalan dan izin saat pertama dibuka, serta jendela Watch & Listen Together, kini berwarna biru Lumi
- Link di komentar kini bisa dipencet: link YouTube dibuka di Lumi, link lain di browser
- YT Music Lumi: **lagu dari Beranda tidak pernah buntu**. Setelah lagu terakhir (Pilihan cepat, Favorit yang terlupakan, radio, dll.), Next berlanjut dengan lagu serupa
- **Playlist hanya memutar isinya sendiri** (YouTube Lumi & YT Music Lumi, termasuk playlist download): tidak pernah lanjut ke saran. Next di lagu/video terakhir kembali ke yang pertama, dan Ulangi playlist mengulang dari awal
- YT Music Lumi: halaman artis punya **Lagu teratas** yang bisa digeser kanan-kiri dengan tombol **Putar semua**. Lagu yang diputar dari sini tetap lagu artis itu dan diulang dari awal saat habis
- YT Music Lumi: tombol **Putar semua** di Pilihan cepat dan rak lagu lainnya
- YT Music Lumi: tarik Beranda ke bawah untuk memuat pilihan lagu baru
- YT Music Lumi: **tekan lama** lagu atau playlist di Beranda untuk Tambahkan ke playlist, Download, dan Bagikan
- YT Music Lumi: halaman lanjutan dari Beranda (Dengarkan lagi, Rilis baru, dll.) kini tersusun ke bawah, dan kotak Dengarkan lagi selalu terisi penuh
- YT Music Lumi: menu **Histori** di tab Anda (sama dengan histori YouTube Music asli), lengkap dengan titik tiga Tambahkan ke playlist, Download, Bagikan
- YT Music Lumi: lirik YouTube Music kini ikut bergerak (juga di PiP); bila YouTube hanya punya teks biasa, waktunya diperkirakan dari durasi lagu
- Watch & Listen Together: bila diizinkan host, anggota bisa memilih lebih dulu meski host belum memutar apa pun. Bila tidak diizinkan, anggota tidak bisa memilih, menggeser durasi, atau putar/jeda, dan muncul pemberitahuan

### 🐞 Perbaikan
- Pencarian dari YouTube Lumi dan YT Music Lumi kini tercatat di histori pencarian YouTube dan YouTube Music asli
- Live yang tidak bisa diputar ulang: garis merah tetap diam di ujung dan tidak bisa digeser, tidak lagi berulang-ulang
- Pencarian di YT Music Lumi tidak lagi gagal dengan pesan "HTTP 400"
- Video dan live untuk anak (YouTube Kids) kini bisa diputar, dan channel konten anak kini menampilkan isinya
- Shorts di daftar video terkait tampil sebagai rak Shorts
- Video anak yang sebelumnya gagal dengan pesan "Video tidak tersedia" kini bisa diputar
- Hapus dari histori tontonan tidak lagi gagal dan ikut terhapus di YouTube asli
- Login: keyboard tidak lagi tertutup saat saran email muncul
- YT Music Lumi: nama artis di pemutar selalu bisa diketuk untuk membuka halaman artis
- YT Music Lumi: Lagu teratas di halaman artis kini memuat daftar lengkapnya (bukan hanya 5 lagu), dan antrean Berikutnya hanya berisi lagu artis tersebut
- Watch & Listen Together: pemutaran tidak lagi berhenti sendiri lalu mengulang dari awal. Saat host memuat, buffering, atau offline, anggota ikut berhenti di posisi yang sama
- Watch & Listen Together: poster lagu tidak lagi hilang dan pindah ke Video tidak lagi kosong
- YT Music Lumi: poster lagu dari album/single tidak lagi kosong di pemutar, dan poster atas halaman (Lagu teratas, Video, Single & EP, dll.) selalu terisi
- YT Music Lumi: semua lagu yang diputar, dari bagian mana pun (Dengarkan lagi, Sampel, pencarian, dll.), kini langsung tercatat di histori YouTube Music asli, dan layar Histori langsung diperbarui
- Live yang dijeda lama tidak lagi berhenti di layar hitam
- Download playlist kini menyimpan semua isinya saja, tanpa video saran yang ikut terdownload
- Mengetuk foto channel yang sedang live kini membuka halaman channel, bukan siarannya

### ⚡ Improvements

- **Download live streams while they're live**: record from the start of the live or from this second. Recording continues until the live ends or you tap "Stop & save"
- **Light and dark themes** in both apps (default: YouTube Lumi light, YT Music Lumi dark)
- **Lumi blue colors** (on by default, can be turned off in Settings): a mostly blue look like the Lumi logo, including the progress bar, Shorts and logo
- **Offline pop-up**: a small pop-up at the bottom when the internet drops, in the app and in PiP mode, then "Back online" once you reconnect
- **"Members only" badge** on videos available only to channel members, and members-only videos and Shorts no longer show up on Home
- **Settings on a single screen**, no scrolling needed
- **Watch & Listen Together**: by default only the room creator picks the video/song, with an option to let room members pick too
- The video title shows in full screen together with the controls
- A **Close** button in the video and music player notification, which also closes the PiP window
- YouTube Lumi: the three-dot menu on playlists now offers Download and Share, like YT Music Lumi
- **Stop a playlist download** from the app (playlist page and Downloads page) or the notification; finished songs/videos are kept
- YT Music Lumi: the search button is now in the top bar and the profile picture in the bottom bar (You tab), like YouTube Lumi
- YT Music Lumi: category boxes in Explore now match Library
- YT Music Lumi: download options now list audio first, then video, and "Download all" for a playlist now saves audio instead of video
- The "Reset links" guide is now clear step-by-step instructions that are no longer cut off
- **Remove from watch history** from the three-dot menu in History. Shorts in History now have a three-dot menu too: Add to playlist, Download, Share and Remove from watch history
- Related videos are now labeled Shorts and Video, like in History
- **Indonesian subtitles**: if a video has no Indonesian subtitles, its original subtitles are automatically translated into Indonesian; if it has no subtitles at all, a "no subtitles" notice appears. Video titles and audio stay original
- Deleting a search in YouTube Lumi and YT Music Lumi (long-press it in search history) now also removes it from the official YouTube and YouTube Music search history
- The welcome and permission screens on first launch, and the Watch & Listen Together window, now use Lumi blue
- Links in comments can now be tapped: YouTube links open in Lumi, other links in the browser
- YT Music Lumi: **songs from Home never run out**. After the last song (Quick picks, Forgotten favorites, radio, etc.), Next continues with similar songs
- **Playlists only play their own items** (YouTube Lumi & YT Music Lumi, including downloaded playlists): they never continue into suggestions. Next on the last song/video goes back to the first, and Repeat playlist starts over
- YT Music Lumi: artist pages have **Top songs** you can swipe left and right, with a **Play all** button. Songs played from there stay with that artist and start over when the list ends
- YT Music Lumi: a **Play all** button on Quick picks and other song shelves
- YT Music Lumi: pull Home down to load new picks
- YT Music Lumi: **long-press** a song or playlist on Home to Add to playlist, Download or Share
- YT Music Lumi: "more" pages from Home (Listen again, New releases, etc.) now scroll vertically, and the Listen again grid is always full
- YT Music Lumi: a **History** entry in the You tab (same as the official YouTube Music history), with a three-dot menu to Add to playlist, Download and Share
- YT Music Lumi: YouTube Music lyrics now move too (also in PiP); when YouTube only has plain text, timing is estimated from the song length
- Watch & Listen Together: when the host allows it, members can pick first even before the host plays anything. When not allowed, members can't pick, seek or play/pause, and a notice appears

### 🐞 Fixes
- Searches from YouTube Lumi and YT Music Lumi are now saved to the official YouTube and YouTube Music search history
- Live streams that can't be rewound: the red bar now stays at the end and can't be dragged, instead of looping
- Searching in YT Music Lumi no longer fails with "HTTP 400"
- Videos and live streams made for kids (YouTube Kids) now play, and kids' channels now show their content
- Shorts in related videos now appear as a Shorts shelf
- Kids' videos that failed with "Video unavailable" now play
- Remove from watch history no longer fails, and the video is removed on the official YouTube too
- Sign-in: the keyboard no longer closes when email suggestions appear
- YT Music Lumi: the artist name in the player can always be tapped to open the artist page
- YT Music Lumi: Top songs on artist pages now load the full list (not just 5 songs), and the Up next queue only has that artist's songs
- Watch & Listen Together: playback no longer pauses by itself and restarts from the beginning. While the host is loading, buffering or offline, members pause at the same spot
- Watch & Listen Together: the song poster no longer disappears, and switching to Video is no longer blank
- YT Music Lumi: songs from albums/singles no longer show an empty poster in the player, and the top poster of pages (Top songs, Videos, Singles & EPs, etc.) is always filled
- YT Music Lumi: every song you play, from anywhere (Listen again, Samples, search, etc.), is now saved right away to the official YouTube Music history, and the History screen updates immediately
- A live stream paused for a while no longer gets stuck on a black screen
- Playlist downloads now save exactly the playlist's items, without suggested videos
- Tapping the picture of a channel that's live now opens the channel page instead of the stream

</details>
