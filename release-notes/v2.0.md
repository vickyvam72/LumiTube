<summary><h2>🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia</h2></summary>

### ⚡ Peningkatan

- **Watch Together** (YouTube Lumi) dan **Listen Together** (YT Music Lumi): nonton atau dengar bareng teman secara bersamaan dengan kode room. Tombolnya ada di bilah atas Beranda, sebelah tombol download
- Pencarian yang kamu ketik kini tersimpan di histori pencarian akun, jadi juga muncul di aplikasi YouTube dan YouTube Music asli
- Ketuk Beranda saat sudah di Beranda untuk langsung kembali ke paling atas
- Layar perkenalan dan izin saat pertama kali dipasang, masing-masing pas dalam satu layar tanpa scroll: hubungkan akun Google atau lewati, lalu aktifkan notifikasi, izin latar belakang, Mulai otomatis (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Infinix), dan pengaturan link
- Garis merah untuk menggeser video lebih mudah diraih di layar penuh dan di Shorts. Di layar penuh hanya muncul bersama tombol kontrol, dan di Shorts tampil tipis di tepi bawah seperti YouTube dengan lingkaran yang hanya muncul saat digeser
- YT Music Lumi: titik tiga di setiap lagu kini berisi Tambahkan ke playlist, Download, dan Bagikan
- YT Music Lumi: titik tiga di playlist dan album (misalnya di hasil pencarian) kini berisi Tambahkan ke playlist (seluruh isinya), Download semua lagu, dan Bagikan. Menu yang sama juga ada di pojok kanan atas halaman album dan playlist orang lain
- Daftar playlist dan Koleksi kini langsung diperbarui otomatis setelah playlist dibuat, diubah, atau dihapus
- Buka aplikasi jauh lebih cepat: versi klien disimpan, beranda disimpan di HP, beberapa klien dicoba bersamaan saat pertama dibuka
- Streaming lebih lancar: download bertahap, ukuran potongan adaptif, lompat ke keyframe, buffer lebih besar
- Video yang terlihat disiapkan lebih dulu, jadi langsung diputar saat diketuk
- Pemutaran tetap jalan saat layar mati
- Coba ulang otomatis dan ganti klien saat stream gagal
- Laporan crash bawaan dengan tombol "Salin laporan"

### 🐞 Perbaikan
- Pemutaran tidak lagi berhenti setelah 1 MB pertama (HTTP 403)
- Lirik tidak lagi macet di "Mencari lirik…" setelah ganti lagu
- Lirik offline tidak lagi hilang di beberapa lagu yang di-download
- Download tidak lagi macet di 100%
- Download playlist kini berisi persis lagu di playlist itu
- Notifikasi download massal tidak lagi menumpuk
- Musik tidak lagi berhenti saat layar mati
- Tombol putar kini memuat ulang stream yang macet
- Filter pencarian kini benar-benar mengubah hasil
- Loading yang berputar tanpa henti di ujung daftar sudah dihilangkan
- Video dan Shorts channel tidak lagi berhenti di halaman pertama
- Status Subscribe kini tampil benar di halaman channel dan hasil pencarian
- Live chat tidak lagi muncul di video yang tidak sedang live
- Urutan playlist kini tersimpan dengan benar
- "Simpan ke playlist" dan "Tonton nanti" tetap berhasil walau panelnya sudah ditutup
- Video dan Shorts yang ditonton kini muncul di histori YouTube
- Error di proses latar belakang tidak lagi membuat aplikasi force close
- Playlist baru (pribadi, tidak publik, maupun publik) langsung muncul di Koleksi dan daftar Playlist
- YT Music Lumi: kembali dari playlist atau album yang dibuka dari hasil pencarian kini kembali ke hasil pencarian, bukan ke kolom ketik
- Memilih saran pencarian (misalnya "tretan" setelah mengetik "tre") kini juga mengubah teks di kolom pencarian
- YT Music Lumi: playlist orang lain (misalnya Radio) tidak lagi menampilkan "Hapus playlist". Playlist yang sudah ada di Koleksi tidak lagi menampilkan "Tambahkan ke playlist"
- Playlist yang sudah dihapus kini langsung hilang dari daftar, tanpa perlu menutup aplikasi
- Hapus playlist kini sinkron dengan YouTube dan YouTube Music asli: hanya playlist milikmu yang bisa dihapus, dan playlist buatan otomatis seperti Recap atau "Dibuat untuk kamu" tidak lagi bisa dihapus
- Beberapa force close acak sudah diperbaiki

<summary><h2>🇬🇧 English</h2></summary>

### ⚡ Improvements

- **Watch Together** (YouTube Lumi) and **Listen Together** (YT Music Lumi): watch or listen with friends in sync using a room code. The button is in the Home top bar, next to the download button
- Searches you type are now saved to your account's search history, so they also appear in the official YouTube and YouTube Music apps
- Tap Home while already on Home to jump straight back to the top
- Welcome and permission screens on first launch, each fitting on a single screen without scrolling: connect a Google account or skip, then allow notifications, background running, Autostart (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Infinix) and link setup
- The red seek bar is easier to reach in full screen and in Shorts. In full screen it only appears together with the controls, and in Shorts it sits thin at the bottom edge like YouTube, with the handle shown only while dragging
- YT Music Lumi: the three-dot menu on every song now offers Add to playlist, Download and Share
- YT Music Lumi: the three-dot menu on playlists and albums (for example in search results) now offers Add to playlist (all of its songs), Download all songs and Share. The same menu is also in the top-right corner of album pages and other people's playlists
- Your playlist list and Library now refresh automatically after a playlist is created, changed or deleted
- Much faster startup: cached client version, disk-cached home feed, parallel clients on cold start
- Smoother streaming: chunked downloads, adaptive chunk size, keyframe seeking, a larger buffer
- Visible videos are prepared in advance, so playback starts almost instantly
- Playback keeps running with the screen off
- Automatic network retry and client switching when a stream fails
- Built-in crash reporter with a "Copy report" button

### 🐞 Fixes
- Playback no longer stops after the first 1 MB (HTTP 403)
- Lyrics no longer get stuck on "Mencari lirik…" (Searching for lyrics…) after switching songs
- Offline lyrics are no longer missing for some downloaded songs
- Downloads no longer freeze at 100%
- A playlist download now contains exactly the playlist's songs
- Batch download notifications no longer pile up
- Music no longer stops when the screen turns off
- The play button now reloads a stalled stream
- Search filters now actually change the results
- Endless loading spinners at the end of lists have been removed
- Channel videos and Shorts no longer stop loading after the first page
- Subscribe status now shows correctly on channel pages and in search results
- Live chat no longer appears on videos that aren't live
- Playlist reordering is now saved reliably
- "Save to playlist" and "Watch later" now work even after the sheet is closed
- Watched videos and Shorts now appear in your YouTube history
- Errors in background tasks no longer crash the app
- New playlists (private, unlisted or public) now show up right away in your Library and Playlists
- YT Music Lumi: going back from a playlist or album opened from search results now returns to the results, not the search box
- Picking a search suggestion (for example "tretan" after typing "tre") now also updates the text in the search box
- YT Music Lumi: other people's playlists (such as Radio) no longer show "Delete playlist", and playlists already in your Library no longer show "Add to playlist"
- Deleted playlists now disappear from the list right away, without restarting the app
- Deleting playlists now stays in sync with the official YouTube and YouTube Music apps: only your own playlists can be deleted, and auto-generated playlists such as Recap or "Made for you" can no longer be deleted
- Several random force closes fixed
